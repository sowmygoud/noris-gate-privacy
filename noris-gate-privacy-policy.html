Noris Gate Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy for the Noris Gate mobile application

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Noris Solutions ("we", "us", or "our") provides the Noris Gate mobile application ("App") for authorised business gate, vehicle, visitor, and register management. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared through the App.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used and your organisation's configuration, the App may process:

  • Account details and user identifiers used for login and authentication.
  • Vehicle registration numbers, transporter details, vehicle entry and exit records, and related gate information.
  • Visitor names, contact numbers, visit details, and information entered in visitor forms.
  • Vehicle or visitor photographs captured or selected in the App, where this feature is enabled.
  • Technical or diagnostic information where collected to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

The actual information collected depends on the App features enabled and the information entered by authorised users.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, record vehicle movements and visitor entries, maintain registers, display records to authorised users, synchronise data with the organisation's business system, maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and provide technical support.

3. Data Sharing

Information may be accessible to the organisation that provides your account and its authorised users. Service providers may process information as necessary to host, maintain, or support the App. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored on the organisation's systems or servers used to provide the App. It is retained for as long as necessary for the relevant business purposes, according to the organisation's requirements and applicable law.

5. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Device Permissions

The App may request camera or photo-library access if needed to capture or select images for vehicle, visitor, or gate records. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Disabling a permission may limit the related feature.

7. Children's Privacy

Noris Gate is a business application and is not designed for children. We do not knowingly design the App to collect personal information from children.

8. Your Requests

For requests to access, correct, or delete information associated with your account or business records, contact your organisation's Noris Gate administrator or reach us using the contact details below. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
